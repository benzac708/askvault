# IT FAQ

## How do I get a new laptop?

Laptop refreshes happen every three years, or immediately if the device is
damaged. Submit a ticket with the asset tag; a replacement arrives within five
business days.

## The VPN drops every afternoon

The VPN is only required for legacy internal admin tools. Most day-to-day work
is done through the browser and does not need the tunnel at all.

## How do I report a phishing email?

Forward the message to the security alias and delete it. Do not reply and do not
open attachments. Reporting a real phishing attempt takes about thirty seconds
and is always appreciated.
