# Engineering Onboarding

## First day

Collect your laptop from IT on the second floor. Your account is provisioned
automatically when you accept the SSO invite. You will need a hardware security
key; registration takes about ten minutes at the IT desk.

## First week

Your onboarding buddy owns a checklist for week one. The only hard requirement is
reading the engineering handbook and running the test suite locally before your
first commit is merged.

## Accounts and access

Production access is requested through Access Hub and approved by your manager.
It is never granted directly, and it expires automatically after 90 days.
